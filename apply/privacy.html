2What we collect

Information you give us. When you apply or book a call: first and last name, email address, phone number, and the answers you provide about your business, including current monthly revenue, your offer, the size of your sales team and the software you use.

Information collected automatically. IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, time on page, the page that referred you, and advertising identifiers such as click IDs.

Information from third parties. Advertising and analytics platforms may pass us campaign and attribution data about how you reached us.

3How we use it

  • To review your application and decide whether to offer a consultation
  • To contact you about that consultation by email, SMS and phone
  • To deliver services you have purchased and to support you as a client
  • To send marketing you have consented to receive, which you can stop at any time
  • To measure and improve our advertising and our website
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

4Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with service providers who process it on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • GoHighLevel for our CRM, pages, email and SMS
  • Typeform for the application form
  • Calendly for scheduling
  • Payment processors, for clients who purchase
  • Advertising and analytics platforms, including Google and Meta
  • Professional advisers, and authorities where the law requires it

5Text messages and email

By giving us your phone number you agree that we may contact you by SMS and by phone about your application and your consultation. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of SMS, or HELP for help. Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link. Opting out of marketing does not stop messages that are necessary to deliver a service you have asked for.

6Cookies and tracking

We and our advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to remember your session, measure campaign performance and show relevant ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may stop working properly.

7How long we keep it

We keep application and contact data for as long as we have a relationship with you and afterwards for as long as we need it to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, or to resolve disputes. When it is no longer needed we delete it or anonymise it.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to opt out of marketing. California residents have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know, to delete, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information as that term is defined by the CCPA.

To exercise any of these, write to jasmine@revenuesystemsmodel.com. We may need to verify your identity before we act.
